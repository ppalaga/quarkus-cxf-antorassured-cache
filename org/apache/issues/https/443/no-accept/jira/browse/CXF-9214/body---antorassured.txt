                    <p>WSDLs and XSDs cannot be loaded from class path on GraalVM native image. </p>

<p>This was caused by this change <a href="https://github.com/apache/cxf/commit/062ebd17a745b2c2028aeae47c0fc92ad0c66a1f#diff-4b89a654670abdd7e7144d7a91184997e9a0347798dc9e9bedc2c2e9aa9b2d13R68" class="external-link" target="_blank" rel="nofollow noopener">https://github.com/apache/cxf/commit/062ebd17a745b2c2028aeae47c0fc92ad0c66a1f#diff-4b89a654670abdd7e7144d7a91184997e9a0347798dc9e9bedc2c2e9aa9b2d13R68</a></p>

<p>adding a list of allowed URL schemes.</p>

<p>The scheme of the URL returned from java.lang.ClassLoader.getResource(String) or similar methods is <tt>resource</tt>. Apparently, it needs to get added to the list.</p>
