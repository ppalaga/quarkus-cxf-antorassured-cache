about exactly what they are getting and from whom.</p><p>This principle is core to trademark law, prohibiting <a href="/policies/trademark-policy/guidelines.html#what-is-likelihood-of-confusion">“likelihood of confusion”</a> while allowing for <a href="/policies/trademark-policy/guidelines.html#what-is-nominative-use">"nominative use"</a> when necessary for accurate identification.</p><div class="callout" data-callout="tip"><div class="callout-title"><div class="callout-title-icon"><svg height="24" width="24"><use href="/assets/icon-symbol.svg#icon-tip"></use></svg></div><div class="callout-title-inner">Tip</div></div><div class="callout-content"><p></p><p>Avoid causing confusion by ensuring that:</p><ul><li>Modified software is clearly labeled as such and not presented as the original software.</li><li>You avoid using logos or other identifiers in ways that suggest an endorsement or official affiliation.</li></ul><p>Examples of what you <em>can</em> say:</p><ul><li>You like the Hibernate software</li><li>You participate in the Hibernate community</li><li>You are providing an unmodified version of the Hibernate software</li><li>You wrote a book describing how to use the Hibernate software.</li></ul></div></div><p>This fundamental requirement, that it is always clear to people what they are getting and from whom, is reflected throughout this Policy. It should also serve as your guide if you are not sure about how you are using the Marks.</p><h2 id="restrictions-on-similar-trademarks" tabindex="-1"><a class="header-anchor" href="#restrictions-on-similar-trademarks">Restrictions on Similar Trademarks</a></h2><p>Trademark law does not allow your use of names or trademarks that are too similar to ours.</p><ul><li><p>You may not use an obvious variation of any of our Marks or any phonetic equivalent, foreign language equivalent, takeoff, or abbreviation for a similar or compatible product or service.</p></li><li><p>You may not use or register, in whole or in part, the Marks as part of your own trademark, service mark, domain name, company name, trade name, product name or service name.</p></li></ul><p>In addition:</p><ul><li><p>You may not use the Marks in association with the use or distribution of software if you are also not in compliance with the copyright license for the software.</p></li><li><p>You agree that you will not acquire any rights in the Marks and that any goodwill generated by your use of the Marks inures solely to our benefit.<sup class="footnote-ref"><a href="#fn1" id="fnref1">[1]</a></sup></p></li></ul><h3 id="uses-we-consider-non-infringing" tabindex="-1"><a class="header-anchor" href="#uses-we-consider-non-infringing">Uses we consider non-infringing</a></h3><h4 id="distribution-of-unmodified-source-code-or-artifacts-packaged-by-us" tabindex="-1"><a class="header-anchor" href="#distribution-of-unmodified-source-code-or-artifacts-packaged-by-us">Distribution of unmodified source code or artifacts packaged by us</a></h4><p>Redistribution of unmodified Commonhaus software (in source code or packaged form, such as executables, libraries, or jar files) is allowed. You may retain the Word Marks and Logos we’ve placed on the software to identify your redistribution if you distribute an official, unchanged Project version.</p><p>You can find files for the Logos and approved designs for packaging on our <a href="https://github.com/commonhaus/artwork">Brand Standards</a> page.</p><div class="callout" data-callout="note"><div class="callout-title"><div class="callout-title-icon"><svg height="24" width="24"><use href="/assets/icon-symbol.svg#icon-note"></use></svg></div><div class="callout-title-inner">Note</div></div><div class="callout-content"><p></p><p>"Artifacts packaged by us" refers to items distributed directly by CF Projects</p></div></div><h4 id="distribution-of-modified-source-code-or-artifacts-packaged-by-you" tabindex="-1"><a class="header-anchor" href="#distribution-of-modified-source-code-or-artifacts-packaged-by-you">Distribution of modified source code or artifacts packaged by you</a></h4><p>If you compile and distribute bitwise identical artifacts from our unmodified source code, you may retain our Word Marks and Logos.</p><p>For modifications (e.g., bug fixes that preserve compatibility with our artifacts), label your artifacts to indicate they were built by you.</p><p>Substantive changes should be treated as a fork, requiring changes to group or artifact identifiers. Avoid using our Logos in these cases, as the modified version originates with you. You may truthfully state that “this software is derived from the source code for Hibernate version 5.2”, as an example.</p><h4 id="statements-about-compatibility-interoperability-or-derivation" tabindex="-1"><a class="header-anchor" href="#statements-about-compatibility-interoperability-or-derivation">Statements about compatibility, interoperability or derivation</a></h4><p>You may use the Word Marks, but not the Logos, to truthfully describe the relationship between your software and ours.</p><p>Our Mark should be used after a verb or preposition that describes the relationship between your software and ours.</p><p>Acceptable usage examples:</p><ul><li><em>&lt;your product name&gt;</em> <strong>is compatible with</strong> Hibernate</li><li><em>&lt;your product name&gt;</em> <strong>works with</strong> JBang</li><li><em>&lt;your product name&gt;</em> <strong>for use with</strong> SDKMAN!</li><li><em>&lt;your product name&gt;</em> <strong>for</strong> SDKMAN!</li><li><em>&lt;your product/company name&gt;</em> <strong>is powered by</strong> Jackson</li><li><em>&lt;your product/company name&gt;</em> <strong>plugin for</strong> JReleaser</li><li><em>&lt;your product/company name&gt;</em> <strong>extension for</strong> Quarkus</li><li><em>&lt;your product/company name&gt;</em> <strong>recipe for</strong> EasyMock</li><li><em>&lt;your product name&gt;</em> <strong>build of</strong> Morphia</li></ul><p>Counter-examples (may cause confusion):</p><ul><li>EasyMock <em>&lt;your product name&gt;</em></li><li>Morphia <strong>by</strong> <em>&lt;your company name&gt;</em></li><li>Feign <strong>-</strong> <em>&lt;your product name&gt;</em></li><li><em>&lt;your product/company name&gt;</em> <strong>-</strong> Quarkus</li></ul><div class="callout" data-callout="note"><div class="callout-title"><div class="callout-title-icon"><svg height="24" width="24"><use href="/assets/icon-symbol.svg#icon-note"></use></svg></div><div class="callout-title-inner">Note</div></div><div class="callout-content"><p></p><p>For plugins, extensions, and other add-on software, the "likelihood of confusion" concern runs counter to how we speak. Let's use Quarkus extensions as an example.</p><p>It may seem normal to say "Alice's Quarkus extension", but "Quarkus extension" can be understood two different ways. That phrase could be interpreted to mean either "Alice's extension for Quarkus" or "an extension for Alice's Quarkus". By using this form, you've introduced a possible misunderstanding about the source of Quarkus. The prepositional form, "Alice's extension for Quarkus" avoids that ambiguity.</p></div></div><h4 id="use-of-trademarks-to-show-community-affiliation" tabindex="-1"><a class="header-anchor" href="#use-of-trademarks-to-show-community-affiliation">Use of trademarks to show community affiliation</a></h4><p>This section outlines the use of our Marks in community contexts, such as avatars, decorative skins, or themes. Guidelines for using our Marks on websites are addressed separately below.</p><p>You are welcome to use our Word Marks and Logos in non-commercial, decorative contexts—such as avatars, themes, or personas—to show your support for the Project, provided that your usage does not imply official representation, endorsement, or affiliation with the Foundation, Project, or community.</p><h4 id="websites" tabindex="-1"><a class="header-anchor" href="#websites">Websites</a></h4><p>You may use the Word Marks and Logos, but not the Trade Dress, on your webpage to show your support for the Foundation or its Projects as long as:</p><ul><li>The website has branding that is easily distinguished from our Trade Dress;</li><li>You own branding or naming is more prominent than any of our Marks;</li><li>The Logos hyperlink to the appropriate Foundation or Project website;</li><li>The site does not mislead customers into thinking that either your website, service, or product is our website, service, or product; and</li><li>The site clearly states that you are not affiliated with or endorsed by the Foundation or the Project.</li></ul><h4 id="publications-and-presentations" tabindex="-1"><a class="header-anchor" href="#publications-and-presentations">Publications and presentations</a></h4><p>You can use the Word Marks in book and article titles, and the Logo in illustrations within the document, as long as the use does not suggest that we have published, endorse, or agree with your work.</p><h4 id="events" tabindex="-1"><a class="header-anchor" href="#events">Events</a></h4><p>You can use the signage in the <a href="https://github.com/commonhaus/artwork">Brand Standards</a> to promote the Foundation and/or its Projects at events.</p><h3 id="uses-for-which-we-are-granting-a-license" tabindex="-1"><a class="header-anchor" href="#uses-for-which-we-are-granting-a-license">Uses for which we are granting a license</a></h3><h4 id="user-groups" tabindex="-1"><a class="header-anchor" href="#user-groups">User groups</a></h4><p>You can use the Word Marks as part of your user group name provided that:</p><ul><li>The main focus of the group is the Foundation, its Projects, or its software;</li><li>Any software or services the group provides are without cost;</li><li>The group does not make a profit;</li><li>Any charge to attend meetings are to cover the cost of the venue, food and drink only.</li></ul><p>Note that the general considerations for all uses, above, still apply. Specifically, you may not use or register the Marks as part of your own trademark, service mark, domain name, company name, trade name, product name or service name.</p><h4 id="promotional-goods" tabindex="-1"><a class="header-anchor" href="#promotional-goods">Promotional goods</a></h4><p>"Promotional goods" are non-software goods that use the Marks and that are intended to advertise, promote, or show membership in the Foundation or its Projects.</p><p>You may make promotional goods for free giveaway at technology-related conferences and events using resources from our <a href="https://github.com/commonhaus/artwork">Brand Standards</a> site.</p><h3 id="uses-we-consider-infringing-without-seeking-further-permission-from-us" tabindex="-1"><a class="header-anchor" href="#uses-we-consider-infringing-without-seeking-further-permission-from-us">Uses we consider infringing without seeking further permission from us</a></h3><p>We will likely consider the following usages an infringement of our Marks:</p><ul><li>Using the Marks as part of a domain name or subdomain.</li><li>Using the Marks on promotional goods for sale.</li><li>Using the Marks as the name for software that has been modified, except as allowed by <a href="#distribution-of-modified-source-code-or-artifacts-packaged-by-you">Distribution of modified source code or artifacts packaged by you</a>.</li></ul><h3 id="reporting-abuse-or-seeking-information" tabindex="-1"><a class="header-anchor" href="#reporting-abuse-or-seeking-information">Reporting Abuse or Seeking Information</a></h3><p>Please <a href="#contact-and-further-information">contact us</a> if you notice misuse of our Marks or have questions about acceptable use.</p><h2 id="policy-modifications-and-updates" tabindex="-1"><a class="header-anchor" href="#policy-modifications-and-updates">Policy modifications and updates</a></h2><p>The Commonhaus Foundation may modify or update this Trademark Policy as needed.
